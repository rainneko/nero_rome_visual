# 侧殿暗室 · Side hall on the islet

## From the book
Book: the side hall on the islet; all windows nailed shut with black wool felt; next to it a lead-plate chamber that gives off white fumes.

## Shape and size
Two storeys, 26 × 15 m, hipped terracotta roof; window openings covered with black felt panels; a small lead-plate shed (10 × 7 m) with a chimney.
