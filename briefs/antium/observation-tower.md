# 观测塔 · Observation tower with the iron spear

## From the book
The highest marble tower of the villa. On top: a thin wrought-iron spear about 10 m long, silver-plated tip (book).

## Shape and size
Square, 13 × 13 m, about 45 m high, nine storeys, small windows, a cornice and a flat top with a parapet; a rod running down one face to the ground (the lightning conductor); the spear centred on top.

## Checklist
- [ ] Total height 50–56 m including the spear
- [ ] Spear thin (≤ 0.2 m), visible against the sky
- [ ] White marble faces with a darker plinth
- [ ] Under 8k triangles
