# 窄桥 · The only bridge to the islet

## From the book
Book: a narrow marble bridge, wide enough for two people side by side, **no railings**; an iron gate and German guards at the bridgehead.

## Shape and size
One high stone arch over a chasm about 60 m wide; deck about 2.7 m wide and 96 m long, about 65 m above the sea; arch springing from the cliff faces near the water; a small gatehouse with an iron gate on the headland end; two statues on each bridgehead.
