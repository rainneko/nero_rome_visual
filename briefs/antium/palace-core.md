# 行宫主殿 · Main palace on the top terrace

## From the book
Imagined from the owner's references (a fortress villa on a headland) and the historic villa at Antium (Nero's birthplace; the villa ran along the shore).

## Shape and size
Courtyard block 104 × 74 m, three storeys, with four corner towers 11 m square and pyramid roofs; a columned loggia facing west to the sea; white walls with a red band at each floor.
