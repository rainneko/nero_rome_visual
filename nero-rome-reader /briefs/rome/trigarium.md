# 公共小跑马场（练车场）· The public practice track, Trigarium (rome_trigarium)

Site: the north-west of the Campus Martius by the Tiber, where charioteers trained (the Trigarium). The page draws a
sand rectangle about 300 × 80 m; you build the things around it as one asset. z = 0 = track level; long axis along X;
front = −Y (the main stand side).
Reference: `circus_small.jpg` — study it: a small circus with a long tiled stand on arches, a starting building with a
small tower at one end, a central barrier with columns and statues, stone walls round the track, a little hut.

## History 【史】
The Trigarium was an open field on the Campus Martius used for training horses and chariots; a small public course,
plainer than the Circus Maximus. (The novel's state of AD 56; no amphitheatre, no later circus buildings.)

## Shape and size (origin = centre of the track)
* A low stone wall round the track: 300 × 80 m rectangle with one rounded end (r 40 at +X), 1.6 m high, 'stone' with a
  'tile' coping; gaps for gates.
* The main stand on the −Y side: 120 m long, a two-storey arcaded stand like the reference (lower arches 'stone',
  upper open gallery with wooden benches and a 'wood' railing, tile roof on posts), 9 m high.
* Starting building at the −X end: 6 stalls with wooden gates, a small square tower (8 × 8, 14 m, pyramid roof) at one
  corner, a gallery on top for the judge.
* Central barrier: 160 m long, 1 m high, 4 m wide, 'stone', with 4 columns carrying bronze statues and two simple
  turning posts (cones on bases, 'stone' — not gilded).
* Stable sheds and a well hut near the starting building; a row of hitching posts.
* Budget ≤ 30 000 triangles. `finish('rome_trigarium', front='-Y')`.
