# Scenic estates: Aventine and the Imperial Academy

Restore a small number of old-style Roman courtyard houses in the city, four on the
Aventine's accessible slope and four around (but outside) the original Imperial Academy
core and separately reserved future wings. Each plot is 30–36 m across, with a continuous two-storey tiled residential ring,
open Greek-columned peristyle, and a visible central fountain/pool. The garden contains
only the fountain and sparse low planting; no residential volume may occupy the court.

## Placement and ground

- Use only naturally suitable, gentle ground. Keep sampled relief at or below 1.8 m;
  keep any raised court/deck exposure at or below 2 m. Prefer shrinking a plot to a
  complete 26–38 m courtyard over cutting a large terrace into the hillside.
- Do not cross or reserve any road, path, fixed building, or other Foot footprint.
  Maintain clearance around every estate and between generated estates.
- Aventine estates belong on the accessible ridge/slope near the existing senatorial
  houses, not in the river valley or on steep vineyard ground.
- Academy estates must stay near the existing academy, outside its rotated 64 × 100 m
  core footprint, future annex/greenhouse plots and approach. Do not replace or delete existing new academy homes.

## Visual and historic checklist

- Two ordinary storeys, roofed in Roman period terracotta; no monumental podium or
  oversized GLB rock pedestal.
- Complete perimeter enclosure, with no gaps or overlapping buildings.
- Greek-style columns are legible around the open court; their eaves/roof line clears
  the garden.
- A pool and fountain are visible from above, with garden planting kept sparse.
- Report each actual placement and every failed candidate with its rejection reason.
