Stay inside the rock foot outline of palrock.js, so no footprint changes and no re-bake. Flights 3.6 to 4 m wide on a solid tuff base or cut into the rock, a landing every 12 to 15 risers, a solid parapet on the open side. Choose the route that ends nearest the street at the north-west corner.
