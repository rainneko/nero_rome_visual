# 配水塔 · Aqueduct terminus and distribution tank on the Palatine (rome_castellum)

Site: the east edge of the Palatine, where the aqueduct bridge from the Caelian hill arrives (the page shows it after
the story's emperor has the aqueduct extended to the palace). The aqueduct comes from the east: asset front = +X.
Reference: `aqueduct_castellum.jpg` — STUDY IT: the last arches of an aqueduct end at a round tower; water pours from
an arched outlet into a sunken basin below.

## History 【史】
Water ran in a covered channel (specus) on top of the arches into a distribution tank (castellum divisorium), where
sluices split it into lead pipes for fountains, baths and the palace (Frontinus, De aquaeductu).
