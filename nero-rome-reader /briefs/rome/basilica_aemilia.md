# 埃米利亚巴西利卡 · Basilica Aemilia with the Porticus of Gaius and Lucius (rome_basilica_aemilia)

Site: the north side of the Forum Romanum, opposite the Basilica Julia; the long south side faces the Forum.
Asset front = −Y (the Forum side; the page rotates it).
References: `forum_plan_LATE.jpg` (only the plan of this basilica), `houses_rich_2.jpg` (arcaded two-storey front).

## History 【史】
* Rebuilt under Augustus (after a fire in 14 BC, restored again in AD 22): a long hall about 90 × 28 m with a raised
  nave; inside columns of coloured marble (africano, pavonazzetto) and a frieze with scenes of early Roman history.
* On the Forum side the two-storey Porticus of Gaius and Lucius (Augustus' grandsons), with a row of shops behind it.

## Shape and size (96 × 32 m; origin = centre; z = 0)
* Front portico along −Y: 6 m deep, two storeys (7 m + 6 m), 16 bays of arches on piers with engaged columns
  ('marble', `arcade` + `column`), cornices, an attic band with a 'red' frieze strip at the top.
* Behind the ground floor of the portico: 16 shops (`taberna_front`, wide dark openings with wooden shutters).
* Behind: the hall, its nave walls rising to 20 m with arched clerestory windows, gable roof along X; aisles with lean
  roofs; back and end walls 'plaster' with a travertine plinth; doors in the short ends.
