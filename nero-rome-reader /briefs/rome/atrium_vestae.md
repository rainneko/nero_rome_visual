# 维斯塔贞女之家 · House of the Vestals, before AD 64 (rome_atrium_vestae)

Site: the south-east corner of the Forum, at the foot of the Palatine, next to the Temple of Vesta. Long axis east–west
(X). Entrance on the west end (toward the temple): asset front = −X.
References: `villa_noble.jpg` (courtyard, pools, statues), `temple_checker_plaza.jpg` (marble colours).

## History 【史】
* The six Vestal Virgins lived here. In AD 56 it is the older, smaller house (it was rebuilt larger after the fire of
  AD 64): a two-storey courtyard house round a long garden court with pools. Statues of the chief Vestals along the
  court are known from the later house; use a few.

## Shape and size (64 × 26 m; origin = centre; z = 0)
* Two-storey ranges (5 m + 4 m) round a long court 50 × 12 m: 'plaster' walls with red bands, tile hip roofs, small
  windows outside (it is a closed house), bigger openings toward the court.
* Court: an Ionic 'marble' colonnade on all four sides (one storey, lean roofs), three rectangular pools on the axis
  (`pool`), low hedges, six statues on pedestals ('marble', 2 m) along the long sides.
* East end: a small shrine (aedicula: 4 columns, pediment) facing west down the court.
* West end: entrance porch with 2 columns and a pediment facing −X.
* Foundation 1 m below z = 0.
