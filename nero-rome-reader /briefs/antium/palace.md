# 行宫主殿 · Upper palace, Nero's residence (ant_palace)

Zone: the top tier of the palace, just below the summit. The grand marble stair arrives at its east front.
References: ref1 (upper palace: blocks round a court, corner towers with pyramid roofs, loggias), ref3 (courts,
peristyles, gardens on the cliff edge), ref4 right side (peristyle court with fountains, red-roofed ranges).

## From the book (quote exactly)
* "皇家行宫沿着悬崖一层一层往上盖，白墙，红瓦，每一层都有一条朝海的长廊。"
* "我在回廊正中的青铜祭坛边上，留了一个一指宽的小缺口。缺口两边各挂一颗磨得很光的纯铜球"
* "两扇镶嵌在青铜框架里的玻璃窗，被装进了尼禄在安齐奥的寝殿。" — the bedchamber looks out over the Tyrrhenian Sea.
* "那天晚上在安齐奥的露台上，尼禄一边切无花果"

## Shape and size
* Quadrangle 84 m (x) × 62 m (y). Outer ranges 13 m deep, two storeys (ground 5.2 m + upper 4.4 m → eaves 9.6 m).
  White plaster, red band at the floor line, travertine plinth, terracotta hip roofs with tile ribs.
* Central cloister (回廊) court about 58 × 36 m with a peristyle (Ionic columns ~5 m, lean-to roofs), paved walks,
  gravel and low hedges. EXACTLY in the centre: the bronze altar — a 3-step marble base (4 × 4 m), a `bronze` altar
  block 1.6 × 1.2 × 1.1 m, and two small `copper` spheres on thin posts beside it.
* Four corner towers 11 × 11 m, 16 m high, pyramid roofs (pitch ~30°), small recessed windows. They rise above the ranges.
* East front (+X) = the main entrance: a temple-front portico, 6 Corinthian columns 8 m tall, pediment with a tiled
  roof, projecting ~7 m from the east range, with 6 marble steps across its whole width.
* West front (−X) = the sea gallery: an open two-level loggia along the whole west range — a ground-floor arcade and
  an upper-floor colonnade with a marble balustrade. Behind it, in the middle of the upper floor, the bedchamber
  (寝殿): two larger windows with `bronze` frames (dark panes).
* North side (+Y, faces the side-hall rock across the chasm): a paved terrace (露台) 40 × 8 m projecting from the
  north range at ground level, with a marble balustrade.
* South side (−Y): plain range with windows and a side door.
* Podium 1.5 m all round (stone) and foundation down to z = −8.
* Front = +X. Budget ≤ 45 000 triangles.
