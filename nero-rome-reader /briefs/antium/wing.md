# 台地翼楼 · Terrace wing with a sea gallery (ant_wing) — a module used four times

Zone: the two lower palace tiers. Two wings flank the grand marble stair on each tier, one facing the north sea,
one facing the south (harbour) side. Offices of the court, guest rooms, the secretariat.
References: ref1 (middle terrace: long two-storey blocks with taller end blocks, cypress rows, a wide stair between),
ref2 (terraced blocks either side of the stair).

## From the book (quote exactly)
* "皇家行宫沿着悬崖一层一层往上盖，白墙，红瓦，每一层都有一条朝海的长廊。" — every tier has a gallery facing the sea.

## Shape and size
* A long two-storey range 60 m (x) × 15 m (y). Ground floor 5 m, upper floor 4.4 m.
* −Y long side = the sea side (the front):
  * ground floor: an arcade of 11 arches (piers, dark back panels behind the openings — a blind arcade on the body),
  * upper floor: an open gallery (loggia) 3.5 m deep under the main roof — 16 columns and a marble balustrade between
    them; the back wall of the loggia has doors/windows.
* +Y long side (faces the inner terrace and the stair): windows on both floors, a central door with a small porch
  (2 columns and a small pediment).
* End pavilions: at each end the last 11 m rise to three storeys (total ≈ 14 m) with their own hip roofs, slightly
  proud (0.6 m) of the range — like the taller end blocks in ref1.
* Red floor bands, white plaster, travertine plinth, terracotta hip roofs with tile ribs; foundation down to z = −6.
* Front = −Y. Budget ≤ 18 000 triangles.
