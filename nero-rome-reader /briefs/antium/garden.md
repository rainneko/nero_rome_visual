# 下层花园 · Circular colonnade (ant_rotunda) and garden portico (ant_portico)

Zone: the lower park of the villa, between the gate and the palace tiers — cypress avenue, flower beds, a round
colonnaded garden on the cliff edge above the harbour, a long portico along the north wall.
References: ref2 (the round double colonnade with a garden inside, flower beds, porticoes, a long portico along the
wall), ref3 (round colonnade court).
The novel does not describe the Antium gardens; this zone follows the references and Roman villa practice.

## ant_rotunda — round double colonnade with a garden inside
* Round platform (stylobate) R = 27 m, 1.2 m high, 4 steps all round (marble).
* Outer ring: 28 Ionic columns, 6 m, at R = 24 m. Inner ring: 24 Ionic columns, 6 m, at R = 19 m.
  Entablatures on both rings, and an annular tiled roof between them (`ring_roof`) sloping outwards.
* Four entrances (N, S, E, W): leave out the column pair on each axis and put a short flight of steps there.
* Inside the inner ring: an open round garden — a round pool R = 8 m with a `bronze` statue on a pedestal in the
  middle, 8 wedge or box flower beds round the pool (low hedges; red, yellow, white flowers), `gravel` paths.
* On the −Y side (the cliff edge, sea view): the platform continues into a semicircular belvedere — a half ring
  4 m wide outside the outer columns (R 27 → 31 m) with a marble balustrade along its edge and two statues.
* Front = −Y. Budget ≤ 20 000 triangles.

Checklist:
- [ ] Two clear concentric rings of columns with a closed ring roof between them
- [ ] Garden, pool and statue readable from above; four entrances
- [ ] Belvedere with balustrade on the −Y side
- [ ] ≤ 20k triangles

## ant_portico — straight garden portico (used along the garden walls)
* 48 m (x) × 6.5 m (y). A solid back wall along +Y (6 m high, plaster, red dado) with 7 shallow round-arched niches,
  a statue in each.
* 17 columns (Tuscan or Ionic, 5 m) along the −Y front, entablature, a lean-to tiled roof sloping to the front,
  closed end walls at both ends.
* Floor raised 0.5 m (paving) with 2 steps along the front; stone benches along the back wall inside.
* Front = −Y. Budget ≤ 8 000 triangles.

Checklist:
- [ ] Columns on the front, niches with statues on the back wall, roof closed at both ends
- [ ] ≤ 8k triangles
