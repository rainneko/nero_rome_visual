# 日耳曼卫营 · Barracks of the German guard (ant_barracks)

Zone: just inside the gate at the land neck, north side of the gate forecourt. Nero's German bodyguard
(Germani corporis custodes【史】) keep the villa; in the book two hundred of them are sent to Antium.

## From the book (quote exactly)
* "调两百个最好的日耳曼人去安齐奥，修铁门，设两道哨卡。守门的队长只对他一个人负责。"

## Shape and size
* A plain military courtyard block 34 m (x) × 22 m (y), two storeys (ground 4.6 m + upper 3.8 m → eaves 8.4 m).
  Plastered `stone` walls (plainer than the palace: no red bands except one at the floor line), small windows,
  terracotta hip roofs with tile ribs.
* Central court 20 × 9 m: `gravel` floor, a wooden gallery (balcony) along the upper floor on all four court sides
  (`wood` posts, rail and floor, lean-to tiled roof), a small shrine (sacellum) on the court's west side —
  a 3 × 3 m aedicula with two columns and a pediment — and a stone water trough.
* Main gate in the middle of the south side (−Y, it faces the gate forecourt): an arched passage 3.2 m wide through
  the range, with a plain wooden double door standing open, and a small tiled porch roof over it.
* On the east end (+X) a small square watch tower rising 4 m above the roof (5 × 5 m), pyramid roof.
* Foundation down to z = −6. Front = −Y. Budget ≤ 9 000 triangles.
