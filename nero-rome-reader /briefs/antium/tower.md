# 观测塔 + 圆形神庙 · Observation tower (ant_tower) and round shrine (ant_tholos)

Zone: the summit at the very tip of the headland — the highest point of the villa. Behind it the cliff falls to the sea.
References: ref1 (tall square tower at the tip, rotunda beside it), ref3 (tower at the cliff corner, round temple).

## From the book (quote exactly)
* "一根三丈长、尖端磨得很锋利、镀了一层银的细熟铁矛，高高立在行宫最高的那座大理石观测塔顶上。"
* "往下引的那一段，是一条粗大的紫铜编织线。它顺着石壁往下走，穿过一节一节用松香和蜂蜡混合做的绝缘套管，最后深深埋进悬崖底下常年泡着海水的湿岩缝里。"
* "最高的那一层，原本是先帝们看海用的观景台。现在台子上立着一根三丈高的铁矛，矛尖镀了银。从港口望上去，像一根刺进云里的针。"
* "石台顶上寒气刺骨……他一个人抱膝蜷缩在石阶的最底层"

So the top is an OPEN stone viewing platform (no roof over it), with stone steps, and the spear stands on it.

## ant_tower — shape and size
* Square shaft 12 × 12 m, white marble ashlar (`stone` walls are fine with `marble` trim), from z = 0 to z = 40.
* Battered tuff plinth z = 0–4 (1 m wider each side at the bottom). Foundation block down to z = −12.
* Thin projecting bands every ~5 m; small arched windows (recessed) two per face per storey above z = 6;
  a door on the east face (+X) at ground level.
* Strong cornice under the platform (z ≈ 39.5).
* Platform (观景台) at z = 40: paved floor on a marble ledge 0.9 m wider than the shaft (it rests on the cornice), ringed by an
  Ionic colonnade — 20 fluted columns 4.3 m tall at 6.35 m from the centre, an entablature with a red frieze, a balustrade
  between the columns; open to the sky (Roman belvedere: Nero and the narrator watch the stars from here). The middle bay
  of the west side stays open for the copper conductor.
* In the middle of the platform: a stepped plinth of 4 marble steps (0.3 m each, from 5 × 5 m down to 2.6 × 2.6 m) — the "石阶".
* The iron spear on top of the plinth: a thin dark `iron` rod, radius ≤ 0.1 m, 10 m tall; the top 0.8 m is a sharpened
  `silver` cone.
* Copper conductor: a `copper` braid (radius ~0.06 m) from the spear base, over the parapet and straight down the
  WEST face (−X, sea side) to z = −12, with small insulator sleeves (`ochre` rings, every 3 m).
* A small stair-head hut on the platform (2.6 × 2.6 m, 2.6 m high, tiled gable roof) in one corner, so the platform
  is reachable from inside.
* Total height ≈ 40 + 1.2 + 10 ≈ 51 m. Front = +X (the door). Budget ≤ 8 000 triangles.

### Checklist
- [ ] Open Ionic colonnade round the platform with entablature and balustrade, open to the sky, stepped plinth in the middle
- [ ] Spear thin, dark, clearly visible against the sky, silver tip
- [ ] Copper line down the west face with insulator rings
- [ ] Darker battered plinth, white shaft, recessed windows, bands and cornice
- [ ] Total height 50–53 m; ≤ 8k triangles

## ant_tholos — round shrine (【史】Antium was famous for its temple of Fortuna, the Fortunae Antiates)
* Round podium R = 8.5 m, 1.6 m high (marble facing), a flight of steps 3 m wide on the east (+X) side.
* 16 Corinthian columns at R = 7.2 m, 7.5 m tall, ring entablature.
* Round cella wall R = 5.4 m (plaster with a red dado), a bronze door facing east.
* Conical tiled roof (rise ≈ 3.5 m) with tile ribs and a bronze finial.
* Foundation below the podium to z = −6. Front = +X. Budget ≤ 8 000 triangles.

### Checklist
- [ ] Columns evenly spaced and standing on the podium (none floating)
- [ ] Cella visible between the columns, door and steps to the east
- [ ] Roof closed, terracotta, finial
